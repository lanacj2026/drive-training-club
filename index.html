Our Workouts

The DR/VE
Difference.

One membership. Three formats. Each designed to complement the others — building endurance, strength, and control all at once.

01 / 03
CYCLE
Rhythm-Based Indoor Cycling

DR/VE CYCLE is built around rhythm. Expect curated playlists, immersive lighting, and beat-driven resistance designed to challenge endurance, build power, and push performance. This full-body cycling experience blends speed, resistance, and intentional movement to challenge both physical and mental limits.

/CYCLE45:CLASSIC

A rhythm-based ride designed to build cardiovascular endurance, power, and stamina. Structured intervals leave you stronger and more resilient every ride. High-energy atmosphere with a curated playlist from start to finish.

/CYCLE45:CLIMB

All the energy of classic CYCLE, with heavier hills, slower resistance pushes, and a stronger focus on power, endurance, and control. Slower tempos and heavier loads target the legs and glutes while building mental grit.

/CYCLE × TRAIN

A hybrid class that combines high-intensity cycling with off-the-bike strength training for a full-spectrum workout. Builds cardiovascular endurance, power, and muscular strength in a single session — maximum efficiency without sacrificing performance.

TRAIN
Progressive Strength Training

This strength-focused format combines structured strength training with intentional conditioning for a total-body, athletic workout. Expect heavier weights, controlled pacing, and progressive programming designed to build power, endurance, and performance. Using dumbbells, barbells, and bodyweight — Train builds muscle, improves stability, and increases overall strength to support long-term results.

/TRAIN45

A total-body strength class centered on progressive overload, functional movement, and athletic performance. Using dumbbells, barbells, and bodyweight — this class builds muscle, improves stability, and increases overall strength. Designed to support long-term results and smarter training.

SCULPT
Low-Impact, High-Control Movement

A rhythm-based sculpt experience focused on control and stability. Low-impact and high-intention — SCULPT classes improve mobility and create long, lean definition while building serious muscular endurance. Each format is designed to complement your higher-intensity training days.

/SCULPT45:TONE

A targeted toning class focused on high-repetition, low-impact movements that lengthen and define muscle. Using lighter weights and bodyweight resistance, Sculpt Tone creates a deep burn while improving posture and muscle definition. Expect continuous, beat-driven sequencing that challenges stability, mobility, and muscular endurance.

/SCULPT45:FLOW

A slower, continuous movement format built to restore, strengthen, and improve mobility through intentional flow, control, and low-impact recovery work. A low-impact class that emphasizes mobility, core strength, and controlled movement. Perfect for active recovery days or pairing with higher-intensity training.

First Timer?

What To
Expect.

⏰
Arrive Early

Come 10–15 minutes before your first class so we can check you in, show you around, and get you set up before the music starts.

👟
Bring The Right Shoes

Cycle shoes included free. Bring tennis shoes for Train. Socks for Sculpt. Our team will help you figure it out at the door.

💧
Bring Water

Bring a water bottle. Towels are complimentary. Water and energy drinks are available for purchase if you forget yours.

🎵
Music-Driven Classes

Every DR/VE class is set to a curated playlist. The music drives the movement — you'll feel it from the first beat.

🤝
Coaches Who Know You

Our coaches learn your name and your goals. Expect real cues, real energy, and modifications when you need them.

🔄
Mix & Match Classes

One membership covers every format. Our cross-training approach means you can cycle Monday, sculpt Wednesday, and train Friday — all on the same plan.
